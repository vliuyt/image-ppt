# Style contract

- Format: full-slide landscape 16:9 images.
- Invariants: restrained editorial palette, strong title hierarchy, generous margins, stable footer and page-number position, no logos or watermarks.
- Approved prototypes: `P01` cover, `P02` dense factual page, `P03` narrative page.
- Page-role variation: maps and timelines may change internal composition while keeping the palette, type character, grid, illustration treatment, and footer fixed.
